# Getting started with Slotwise

Slotwise is online booking for small studios: yoga and pilates studios, salons, tutors, therapists and
anyone else who sells their time by the appointment. This guide gets you from a new account to a working
booking link in about fifteen minutes.

## 1. Set up your studio profile

Go to **Settings → Studio** and fill in your studio name, address, phone number and time zone. The time
zone matters: every appointment is stored in it. See [Time zones](03-time-zones.md) if you have staff or
clients in other zones.

## 2. Add your services

Go to **Services → New service**. For each service set:

- **Name and description**, as clients will see them.
- **Duration**, anywhere from 5 minutes to 8 hours.
- **Buffer time** before and after, for cleaning up or travelling between clients. A 60-minute massage
  with a 15-minute buffer after it blocks 75 minutes of the calendar but shows clients 60.
- **Price**, and optionally a deposit (see [Taking deposits and payments](10-taking-deposits-and-payments.md)).
- **Who performs it**, if you have more than one staff member.

## 3. Set your hours

Your booking page only offers times inside your business hours. See
[Business hours and time off](02-business-hours-and-time-off.md).

## 4. Share your booking link

Your booking page lives at `https://book.slotwise.app/<your-studio>`. Share it on social media, add it to
your email signature, or embed it on your own site with the
[booking widget](04-embedding-the-booking-widget.md).

## Free trial

Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of the Studio plan, no card required. You pick a plan before the
trial ends; see [Plans and pricing](21-plans-and-pricing.md).
