# Changing or cancelling your plan

Only the account **Owner** can change the plan, under **Settings → Billing → Plan**.

## Upgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately. You're charged the **prorated difference** for the rest of the current
billing period, and the new features are available straight away.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect **at the end of the current billing period**. Before then, Slotwise checks what
won't fit on the smaller plan and tells you. For example:

- More staff than the new plan allows: choose who to deactivate. Their history is kept.
- A custom domain (Pro only): the booking page goes back to `book.slotwise.app/your-studio`.
- API keys (Pro only): they stop working when the downgrade takes effect.

Nothing else changes. Your clients, bookings and history are all kept.

## Cancelling

Click **Cancel subscription**. Your account stays fully usable until the end of the period you've paid for.
After that:

- Your booking page stops taking bookings.
- Your data is kept for **90 days**, during which you can reactivate or export it (see
  [Exporting your data](20-exporting-your-data.md)).
- After 90 days, everything is permanently deleted.

For refunds of subscription charges, see [Invoices and receipts](23-invoices-and-receipts.md).

## Pausing instead

Seasonal business? Owners can **pause** for up to 3 months per year for $5 a month. The booking page goes
offline but everything else stays as it is.
