# Invoices, receipts and VAT

This article is about **your Slotwise subscription**. For payments your clients make to you, see
[Taking deposits and payments](10-taking-deposits-and-payments.md).

## Getting invoices

Every subscription charge produces an invoice, emailed to the billing email address. All past invoices
can be downloaded as PDFs under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.

To send invoices to your accountant or a shared inbox, change the **billing email** on the same page.

## Adding a VAT or GST number

Enter your company name, address and tax ID (VAT, GST or ABN) under **Settings → Billing → Tax details**.
Future invoices show them. If you're an EU business with a valid VAT number, the reverse charge applies and
we stop adding VAT.

Need an existing invoice reissued with your tax details? Add the details first, then click **Regenerate**
next to the invoice. You can regenerate invoices from the last 12 months.

## Refunds on the subscription

- **Monthly plans** are not refunded for part of a month. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of
  the period.
- **Annual plans** can be refunded in full within **14 days** of the charge, including the renewal charge.
  Contact support to request this.

## Failed subscription payments

If your card fails, we retry three times over 7 days and email the Owner each time. Update the card under
**Settings → Billing → Payment method**. After the final retry the account is paused (not deleted) until
payment succeeds.
