# Time zones and daylight saving

## Your studio time zone

Every studio has one time zone, set in **Settings → Studio**. Your business hours and your calendar use it.
If the times on your calendar look shifted by a whole number of hours, check this setting first: it is
the most common cause.

## What clients see

The booking page shows times in the **client's own time zone**, detected from their device, and labels them
("Times shown in Europe/London"). A client in London booking a studio in Lisbon sees London times, and the
appointment lands at the correct time on your calendar.

Clients who travel can switch the displayed time zone with the selector under the calendar.

## Times in emails and SMS

Confirmation and reminder messages use the studio time zone by default. If many of your clients are
remote (online lessons, for example), turn on **Settings → Notifications → Show times in the client's time
zone**. Messages then include the client's local time and the zone name.

## Staff in other time zones

Staff who work remotely can set their own time zone under **Team → (staff member) → Time zone**. Their
working hours are then entered in their zone.

## Daylight saving

Slotwise adjusts for daylight saving automatically. Appointments keep their local wall-clock time when the
clocks change: a 9:00 class stays at 9:00.
