# Exporting your data

Your data is yours. You can export it at any time, on any plan.

## Quick exports

Each list has an **Export** button that downloads what you see, with your current filters, as CSV:

- **Clients**: names, contact details, notes, tags, first and last visit.
- **Calendar → List view**: bookings in the date range you pick.
- **Reports → Payments**: every payment, refund and fee, with Stripe payout dates. Your accountant will
  want this one.

## Full account export

For everything at once, the Owner can go to **Settings → Data → Export everything**. We put together a ZIP
file with all clients, bookings, payments, services, staff and message history as CSV files, and email
a download link within **24 hours**. The link works for 7 days.

## After you cancel

If you cancel your subscription, your data is kept for **90 days** in case you change your mind. You can
still log in and export during that time. After 90 days it is permanently deleted. See
[Changing or cancelling your plan](22-changing-or-cancelling-your-plan.md).

## Deleting a single client's data

To meet a client's request to be forgotten, open **Clients → (client) → Delete client and data**. This
removes their details and anonymises their past bookings, so your reports still add up.
