# Business hours, breaks and time off

Clients can only book inside the hours you set. There are three tools for controlling availability.

## Weekly hours

**Settings → Hours** sets the regular opening hours for the whole studio. Each staff member can narrow
these under **Team → (staff member) → Working hours**. A stylist who only works Tuesday to Thursday
afternoons should have exactly those hours set, even if the studio is open all week.

Recurring breaks, such as a daily lunch break, are added on the same screen with **Add break**.

## Blocking time off

To take a single slot or a few days out of the calendar, open **Calendar**, drag across the time you want to
block and choose **Block time**. Give it a reason if you like ("dentist", "training"); clients never see it.
For a holiday spanning several days, use **Calendar → Block time → Multiple days**.

Blocking time does not cancel bookings that already exist in that period. Slotwise warns you about them so
you can reschedule each one.

## Studio closures

Public holidays and other days when the whole studio is closed go in **Settings → Hours → Closures**. A
closure removes the day from every staff member's availability at once.

## How far ahead clients can book

Under **Settings → Booking rules** you can set the minimum notice (for example, no bookings less than two hours
ahead) and the booking window (for example, no more than 60 days ahead).
