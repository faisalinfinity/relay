# Staff accounts and roles

## Inviting staff

Go to **Settings → Team → Invite**, enter their name and email, and choose a role. They get an email to set
their password. Each person needs their own login. Please don't share accounts: shared logins can't have
their own calendar sync or two-factor authentication.

The number of staff you can add depends on your plan: **1 on Solo, up to 10 on Studio, unlimited on Pro**.
Deactivated staff don't count towards the limit.

## Roles

| Role           | Can do                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Owner**      | Everything, including billing, plan changes and deleting the account. One per studio.                  |
| **Manager**    | All calendars, clients, services and reports, and refunds. No billing.                                 |
| **Staff**      | Their own calendar, and the clients they have seen. Can't see other staff's clients or reports.        |
| **Front desk** | Book, move and cancel appointments on every calendar, and check clients in. No reports and no refunds. |

A new stylist or therapist who should only see their own appointments and clients should get the **Staff**
role.

## Changing someone's role

**Settings → Team → (person) → Role**. Changes apply the next time they load a page.

## When someone leaves

**Settings → Team → (person) → Deactivate**. They lose access immediately. Their past appointments stay
on record. Reassign any future appointments to another staff member first; Slotwise lists them for you
when you deactivate.

## Transferring ownership

Only the current Owner can make someone else the Owner, under **Settings → Team → Transfer ownership**.
