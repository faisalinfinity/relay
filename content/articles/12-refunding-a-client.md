# Refunding a client

## Issue a refund

1. Open the booking from the calendar or from **Clients → (client) → Bookings**.
2. Under **Payments**, click **Refund**.
3. Choose **Full refund** or enter an amount for a **partial refund**, add an optional note for the client,
   and confirm.

The client gets an email confirming the refund. Owners and Managers can issue refunds; Staff and Front
desk roles can't (see [Staff accounts and roles](17-staff-accounts-and-roles.md)).

## How long it takes

Refunds are sent to the client's card straight away, but banks take **5 to 10 business days** to show them
on the client's statement. If a client says they haven't received it, send them the refund reference from
the booking's payment history. Their bank can trace it.

## Fees on refunds

Stripe doesn't return its processing fee when you refund. The Slotwise platform fee **is** returned on a full
refund.

## Cancelled bookings

Cancelling a booking does **not** refund it automatically. Your cancellation policy decides whether the
deposit is kept. If you want to return it, cancel the booking and then refund it as above.

## Refunds older than 180 days

Card networks don't allow refunds to a card more than 180 days after the payment. For older payments, refund
the client another way, for example by bank transfer, and record it in the booking's notes.
