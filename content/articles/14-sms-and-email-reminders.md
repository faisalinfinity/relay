# SMS and email reminders

Reminders cut no-shows more than anything else you can switch on.

## Default reminders

Out of the box, every client gets:

- a **confirmation email** as soon as they book
- a **reminder email 24 hours** before the appointment

You can add an **SMS reminder**, for example 2 hours before. Set this up under **Settings → Notifications →
Reminders**. You can have up to three reminders per booking, each by email or SMS, at any time from 15
minutes to 7 days before.

## SMS credits

Each plan includes SMS credits every month: **100 on Solo, 500 on Studio, 2,000 on Pro**. One message of up to
160 characters uses one credit; longer messages use two. Unused credits don't roll over.

Need more? Buy a pack of 500 extra credits for $10 under **Settings → Billing → SMS credits**. Bought credits
never expire. When you run out, reminders that would have gone by SMS are sent by email instead.

## Where SMS works

SMS reminders can be sent to numbers in the **US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand and the EU**.
Clients elsewhere get email reminders only.

## Clients can opt out

Clients can reply **STOP** to any SMS to stop receiving them. See
[SMS not delivered](16-sms-not-delivered.md).

To change what the messages say, see
[Customising notification templates](15-customising-notification-templates.md).
