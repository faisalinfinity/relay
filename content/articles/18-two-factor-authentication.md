# Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from your phone as well as your password when you log in.
We strongly recommend it for everyone who can see client details or payments.

## Turn it on

Go to **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app
(Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy or similar), and enter the code it shows.

You'll then see **10 recovery codes**. Save them somewhere safe, such as a password manager or printed and filed.
Each code works once, in place of a phone code.

Owners can require 2FA for the whole team under **Settings → Security → Require 2FA**.

## Lost your phone?

1. **Use a recovery code.** On the login screen, choose _Use a recovery code_. Once you're in, set up 2FA again
   on your new phone. This generates new recovery codes.
2. **No recovery codes, and you're not the Owner?** Ask your studio Owner or a Manager to go to
   **Settings → Team → (you) → Reset 2FA**. You can then log in with just your password and set 2FA up
   again.
3. **You're the Owner and you have no recovery codes?** Email support **from the email address on the
   account**. We verify your identity before removing 2FA. This takes **1 to 2 business days**, and we
   will never do it over chat or phone, because that is exactly what an attacker would ask for.
