# Connecting Google Calendar

Two-way sync with Google Calendar keeps your personal events and your Slotwise bookings in one place.

## Connect

1. Go to **Settings → Calendar → Google Calendar** and click **Connect**.
2. Sign in with the Google account that holds your calendar and allow access.
3. Choose which calendar Slotwise should **write bookings to**, and which calendars it should **check for
   busy times**.

Each staff member connects their own Google account from their own login. One Slotwise staff member can
connect one Google account.

## What syncs

- New Slotwise bookings appear in your chosen Google calendar within about two minutes, including the
  client's name and the service.
- Events in your "check for busy times" calendars block those times on your booking page, so clients can't
  book over your dentist appointment. Only the time is read. Event titles and details stay private.
- Rescheduling or cancelling in Slotwise updates the Google event. Moving a Slotwise event inside Google does
  **not** move the booking. Make changes in Slotwise.

## Disconnecting

**Settings → Calendar → Google Calendar → Disconnect** stops the sync. Events already copied to Google stay
there unless you tick _Remove Slotwise events from Google_.
