# Cancellation, rescheduling and no-show fees

Set your policy once in **Settings → Policies** and Slotwise enforces it on every booking.

## Letting clients cancel or reschedule

Every confirmation email has _Reschedule_ and _Cancel_ links. You choose how late clients can use them
with the **cancellation window**, for example 24 hours before the appointment. Inside the window the links
stop working and the client is asked to contact you directly.

## Late cancellation fees

Turn on **Charge a late cancellation fee** and set an amount or a percentage of the service price. The fee
is charged to the client's saved card when they cancel inside the window, or when you mark the booking as
_Late cancel_.

To charge a fee, Slotwise needs a card on file. That happens automatically when the service takes a deposit
or full payment. Otherwise turn on **Require a card to book** under **Settings → Payments**. The card is saved
but not charged at booking time.

## No-show fees

Mark a booking as **No-show** from the calendar to charge the no-show fee, which you set separately on the same
screen. Nothing is charged automatically for no-shows; it is always your decision per booking.

## Showing the policy to clients

Your policy text is shown on the booking page before the client confirms, and the client has to tick a
box to accept it. Edit the wording under **Settings → Policies → Policy text**.
