# Connecting Outlook and Microsoft 365 calendars

Slotwise syncs with Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 calendars the same way it syncs with Google: bookings
are written to your calendar, and your busy times block your booking page.

## Connect

Go to **Settings → Calendar → Outlook** and click **Connect**, then sign in with your Microsoft account
and choose your calendars.

## "Need admin approval"

Work and school Microsoft 365 accounts are often set up so that only an IT administrator can approve
new apps. If you see a **Need admin approval** screen after signing in, nothing is wrong with your account.
Your organisation just needs to approve Slotwise once:

1. In **Settings → Calendar → Outlook**, click **Send to my admin**. This gives you a link to forward.
2. Your Microsoft 365 administrator opens the link and approves **Slotwise Calendar Sync**. It asks only
   for calendar read/write permissions.
3. Click **Connect** again. It now goes straight through.

After approval, everyone else in your organisation can connect without asking again.

## Shared and delegated calendars

You can sync a shared calendar (for example a team "Bookings" calendar) as long as your account has edit
access to it in Outlook. If that access is removed later, sync stops with error **SW-1043**. See
[Calendar sync troubleshooting](09-calendar-sync-troubleshooting.md).
